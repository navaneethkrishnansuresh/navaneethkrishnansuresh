## 🔬 Research & Publications

### [Micro-Expressions with Swin-T on CASME II](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11508092)

**2025 IEEE 1st International Conference on Recent Trends in Computing and Smart Mobility (RCSM)**

Research on micro-expression recognition using **Swin Transformer Tiny (Swin-T)** on the CASME II dataset.

The work compared:

- **Spatial implementation:** Lightweight Conv-Norm-GELU blocks added to Swin-T to capture subtle local facial features.
- **Temporal implementation:** GRU/1D-Conv head aggregating motion information across onset–apex–offset frames.
- **Pretraining:** Compared ImageNet-1K and RAF-DB to study general-purpose versus domain-specific prior knowledge.

**Results:** **91.28% accuracy** and **0.9087 macro-F1** on CASME II.

**[Read the paper on IEEE Xplore →](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11508092)**
