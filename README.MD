## Research & Publications

### [Micro-Expressions with Swin-T on CASME II](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11508092)

**2025 IEEE 1st International Conference on Recent Trends in Computing and Smart Mobility (RCSM)**

Research on micro-expression recognition using **Swin Transformer Tiny (Swin-T)** on the CASME II dataset.

The work compared spatial and temporal approaches, including lightweight Conv-Norm-GELU blocks and GRU/1D-Conv temporal heads across onset–apex–offset frames. It also compared **ImageNet-1K and RAF-DB pretraining** to study the impact of general-purpose versus domain-specific prior knowledge.

**Results:** **91.28% accuracy** and **0.9087 macro-F1** on CASME II, with the temporal implementation using RAF-DB pretraining outperforming the spatial implementation and previous state-of-the-art approaches evaluated in the study.

[Read the Paper on IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11508092)
